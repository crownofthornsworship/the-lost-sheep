# The Lost Sheep — Improvement Roadmap

## Product goal

Keep the gentle, readable shepherding game that already works, while preventing the later lands from turning into frantic luck. Difficulty should grow through interesting choices, not overwhelming speed or enemy counts.

## Phase 1 — Difficulty and recovery

- Validate the new difficulty modes and later-level rebalance on touch and keyboard.
- Cap simultaneous threats and add short warning tells before wolf charges.
- Give returning sheep a brief safe period so rescues cannot immediately unravel.
- Add generous checkpoints and a quick retry that preserves the player's selected mode.
- Tune each land with target completion times, wolf pressure, and sheep spread rather than one global speed curve.

## Phase 2 — Better play

- Give the shepherd call a visible radius, cooldown cue, and stronger audiovisual response.
- Add terrain that changes decisions: gates, bridges, tall grass, streams, and safe pens.
- Introduce optional stars for gentle mastery goals such as no lost sheep, quick rescue, or all discoveries.
- Add calm bonus encounters between difficult lands instead of stacking constant danger.

## Phase 3 — Art and animation

- Replace placeholder-feeling movement with directional shepherd, sheep, and wolf animation sets.
- Add readable idle, alert, run, rescue, celebrate, and tired poses.
- Give every region a distinct palette, landmark, ambient motion, and illustrated story card.
- Improve hit feedback without scary imagery; keep the tone appropriate for young children.

## Phase 4 — Content

- Build lands around distinct mechanics rather than larger enemy swarms.
- Add friendly helpers, lost lamb variants, optional discoveries, and story moments.
- Finish with a multi-stage rescue finale that tests learned skills without a sudden difficulty spike.

## Release gate

- Complete every land on Easy, Story, and Challenge modes.
- Test portrait and landscape layouts on small phones, tablets, and desktop.
- Verify touch targets, spoken story cards, reduced motion, sound controls, saves, reset, and offline-safe local progress.
- Refresh `og.png`, metadata, README, Light & Life Games, and Salt & Light Web Studio for the release.
